# Content guardrails

## Do not invent
- project outcomes;
- numerical results;
- tools;
- clients;
- ownership;
- proficiency levels;
- employment scope;
- certifications;
- location or availability statements.

## Do not publish
- the confidential AI project name;
- phone number;
- Qwell;
- Databricks;
- Snowflake;
- McKinsey Forward;
- unfinished SQL certification;
- internship-seeking copy;
- `Client: SME fitness businesses`;
- old Technologies of Trust bullets.

## Do not rewrite
User-facing English copy in `02_EXACT_COPY_EN.md` is approved. Use it exactly unless a purely technical constraint requires punctuation normalization. Report any such change rather than silently rewriting.

## Supporting materials
`source_materials/Кейсы.docx` and the master profile are evidence repositories only. For this phase, project pages are skeletons. Do not extract extra content into the live site without approval.
