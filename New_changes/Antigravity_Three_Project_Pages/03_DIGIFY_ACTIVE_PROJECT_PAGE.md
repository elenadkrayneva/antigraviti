## Project at a Glance

### Context

A B2B marketing concept created for SME fitness businesses with limited structured digital-marketing capabilities.

### Core question

**How can a new B2B marketing service attract and convert SME fitness businesses through a structured digital acquisition funnel?**

### Execution

- market and target-segment analysis;
- positioning and value proposition;
- customer journey and funnel design;
- HubSpot website and lead capture;
- Google Ads Search and Display;
- campaign and conversion analysis.

### Test conditions

Approximately €25 in controlled Google Ads spend over about one month.

Do not present the target market as a real client.

## Section 1 — Business Challenge

Many small and medium-sized fitness businesses lacked a structured approach to digital acquisition. The project therefore needed to create a differentiated B2B service proposition and connect it to a realistic path from initial awareness to lead submission.

Highlight:

> How can a new B2B marketing service attract and convert SME fitness businesses through a structured digital acquisition funnel?

## Section 2 — Strategic Foundation

Use three subsections.

### Market gap

The team identified a gap between the growth needs of SME fitness businesses and their ability to build consistent digital-marketing systems.

### Target segment and positioning

The project defined a target segment, customer needs, value proposition, and service positioning designed around the constraints of smaller fitness businesses.

### Pricing logic

Pricing was developed as part of the service concept and aligned with the target market and proposed offer.

## Section 4 — Paid Acquisition

### Search and Display

### Channel comparison

Search and Display performance were compared to understand how channel intent, targeting, and bidding decisions affected acquisition efficiency.
