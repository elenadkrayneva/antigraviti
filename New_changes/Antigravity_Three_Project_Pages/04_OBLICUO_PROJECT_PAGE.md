## Project at a Glance

### Context

Oblicuo is a listening bar inspired by Japanese Hi-Fi culture, combining music, design, craft drinks, and a curated atmosphere.

### Core question

**How could the venue increase Tuesday–Thursday visits without losing its premium, intimate, and locally relevant identity?**

### Research

- 16 in-venue customer interviews;
- competitor and platform analysis;
- five-persona framework;
- customer journey analysis;
- behavioural barriers and decision triggers.

### Deliverable

A data-informed growth and communication strategy, a three-month implementation plan, and a KPI framework connecting digital activity to weekday visits.

## Section 1 — Business Challenge

Oblicuo had strong brand authenticity but inconsistent early-week attendance and limited structured digital strategy. The goal was not maximum reach. Broad visibility could attract poorly matched audiences and weaken the experience that differentiated the venue.

Highlight:

> How could the venue increase Tuesday–Thursday visits without losing its premium, intimate, and locally relevant identity?

## Section 2 — Research Approach

Use three subsections.

### Customer interviews

The team conducted 16 in-venue interviews to understand visit motivations, customer expectations, behavioural patterns, and the triggers behind choosing a weekday venue.

### Competitor and platform analysis

Competitor positioning and social-media activity were reviewed to identify communication gaps and understand how comparable venues shaped expectations.

### Segmentation and journeys

The findings were translated into a five-persona framework and customer journeys covering awareness, consideration, visit decision, experience, and loyalty.

## Section 3 — Key Insights

Display four insight cards:

1. **Social media often validated a choice rather than created initial discovery.**
2. **The main barrier was uncertainty about crowd level, noise, seating, and atmosphere.**
3. **Different audiences valued the Hi-Fi concept for different reasons.**
4. **Weekday demand required predictability rather than hype.**

Do not present assumptions as measured post-campaign outcomes.

## Section 4 — Priority Segment

Use:

The priority weekday segment was the **After-Work Neighbor**: a nearby professional looking for one good drink and a comfortable conversation without planning a full night out.

The broader framework included four customer personas and one collaborator persona.

The page should show the persona framework visually, but the After-Work Neighbor should receive the strongest emphasis because Elena developed its detailed implementation layer.

## Section 5 — Strategy

Use:

The strategy repositioned social media as a **risk-reduction tool**.

Content should reduce uncertainty by showing:

- the early-evening atmosphere;
- available seating and conversation-friendly conditions;
- proximity and convenience;
- familiar staff and drinks;
- a repeatable weekday ritual.

The objective was to convert local consideration into spontaneous Tuesday–Thursday visits while protecting the venue’s hidden-gem identity.
