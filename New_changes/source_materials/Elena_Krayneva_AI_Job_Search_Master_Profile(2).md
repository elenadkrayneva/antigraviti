# 11. Vacancy match framework

For each vacancy, score the candidate on the following dimensions.

## 11.1 Weighting

- **Core responsibilities:** 30%
- **Required analytical / technical skills:** 20%
- **Business-domain relevance:** 15%
- **Transferable experience:** 15%
- **Seniority fit:** 10%
- **Language, location, and legal feasibility:** 10%

## 11.2 Recommended interpretation

- **85–100%:** Strong apply. Most important requirements are evidenced.
- **70–84%:** Apply. Good transferable match with manageable gaps.
- **55–69%:** Selective apply. Needs a compelling angle or the role must be genuinely junior.
- **Below 55%:** Usually deprioritise unless the company or learning opportunity is unusually valuable.

## 11.3 Automatic red flags

- 3+ years of direct experience in the exact function is mandatory.
- Advanced SQL/Python or production analytics is a strict must-have.
- Native/fluent local language is mandatory and English-only is not accepted.
- Internship duration is incompatible with the 6-month requirement.
- Role requires unrestricted work authorisation that is not available.
- Senior independent ownership of large budgets, P&L, or team management.
- The role is predominantly sales, graphic design, software engineering, or content production.
